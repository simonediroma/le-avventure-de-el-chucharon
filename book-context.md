# Visual Bible — Le Avventure de El Chucharon

## Art Style
Italian picture book for children 0–3 years old.
- Warm watercolor and gouache technique, editorial children's book illustration
- Soft linework, Pixar-level expressiveness
- Expressive characters — emotions readable at a glance, no ambiguity
- Picture-book proportions: large heads, round bodies for babies and toddlers
- Backgrounds: essential and uncluttered — never compete with characters
- Overall feel: warm, sunny, slightly magical, Mediterranean

## Color Palette
- Warm, saturated tones: golden yellow, amber orange, terracotta red, deep green
- Skin tones: warm Mediterranean olive/tan
- Backgrounds: soft and simple, never cold
- Sunset/evening light: the dominant light source in outdoor scenes
- Domestic interiors: warm, golden-hour indoor light
- Chucharon accent colors: vivid Mexican palette (bright red, green, yellow sombrero)

## MANDATORY Text Zone — every single illustration
Every illustration in this book MUST have this layout:
- **Upper 70%**: all action, all characters, all composition — full bleed illustration
- **Bottom 30%**: smooth, seamless gradient fade to warm cream white (#FFF8F0)
- The fade must feel natural and organic, not like a hard bar or a separate element
- The cream area must be clean, bright, and completely empty — it is where text is printed
- NO exceptions. This applies to every illustration without exception.

## NO Text / NO Dialogue Rule — every single illustration
- NO speech bubbles
- NO thought clouds
- NO caption boxes
- NO text, letters, words, or numbers of any kind anywhere in the image
- Dialogue and emotions are conveyed ONLY through facial expressions, body language,
  gestures, and visual storytelling
- Characters can have open mouths (singing, shouting, laughing) — but no words appear

## Chucharon Invisibility Rule
In every scene where adult parents are present:
- Parents NEVER look directly at Chucharon or react to him as a magical being
- Parents always direct their gaze at their child, at each other, or at the environment
- Only the baby/toddler protagonist notices Chucharon (or reacts to him as a "flying pacifier")
- Chucharon acts freely in the scene — flying, dancing, drawing in the air — but parents
  behave as if he is not there or is just a background object
- Exception: in the Epilogue, Elena's father mentions the ciuccio as a physical object,
  not as a magical character

## Recurring Settings

### Park — parco (Prologo and Epilogo)
Italian public playground in summer.
- Equipment visible: slide (scivolo), swings (altalena), seesaw (salta salta)
- Long straight paved pathway (vialetto) cutting through the park
- Sky: deep orange/golden sunset gradient
- Light: warm, late-afternoon, directional sun casting long golden shadows
- Ground: dry summer grass, sandy patches under equipment
- Trees: Italian park trees, dark silhouettes against orange sky
- Atmosphere: end of a hot summer day, quiet, magical light

### Bedroom — camera (Capitolo 1)
Parents' bedroom in an Italian apartment.
- Large double bed with soft domestic linens (warm whites, creams, light blues)
- Simple headboard, pillows stacked
- Baby lies in the center of the bed — always horizontal
- Parents stand or kneel on either side of the bed
- Light: warm domestic, soft late-afternoon coming through a window
- Keep furniture minimal — bed is the entire composition anchor

### Living Room — soggiorno (Capitolo 2)
Italian home living room.
- Handsome walnut wood chair — beautiful, solid, high quality — with visible bite marks
- Sofa in background
- Warm domestic atmosphere, afternoon light
- Not cluttered — keep it minimal Italian home

### Kitchen / Dining — cucina (Capitolo 3)
Italian kitchen and dining area.
- High chair (seggiolone) with tray at the table
- Table: simple, domestic, bowl of food cooling on it
- Italian kitchen atmosphere — warm, homey, functional
- Baby in high chair is the central visual anchor

### Mexican Landscape — messico (drawn magically in the air by Chucharon)
This is NOT a real location — it is a magical glowing drawing that Chucharon
creates in mid-air by spinning his sombrero:
- Distant mountain silhouettes
- Deep orange sunset sky with dramatic clouds
- Low, twisted desert trees
- Dirt road disappearing toward the horizon
- The drawing appears as luminous colored lines floating in the air
- The surrounding scene (bedroom, park) is still visible behind it

### Maya Pyramid — piramide (drawn magically in the air by Chucharon)
Chichén Itzá style step pyramid, also drawn in glowing lines in mid-air by Chucharon.
- Recognizable stepped pyramid silhouette
- Appears as glowing, magical floating lines — not a solid real structure
- Baby character stares at it in wide-eyed wonder, mouth open, silent
