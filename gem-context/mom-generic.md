# Mom — Visual Spec (all chapters)

## Who
Mother of the baby protagonist. Same archetype across all chapters — each chapter has its own family, but all share this visual type. Young Italian mom: competent, warm, the calm and reasonable counterpart to the dad's creative chaos.

## Age & Physical Appearance
- 28–35 years old
- Italian, Mediterranean features: dark or chestnut brown hair (long or tied back practically)
- Average build, normal proportions — not thin, not heavy
- Skin: warm Mediterranean olive/tan
- Hair: practical — often in a ponytail or loose bun, or down if the scene is relaxed

## Clothing (always casual, always summer)
- Light summer dress, or t-shirt with linen trousers
- Sandals — comfortable, practical
- Colors: warm but not garish (terracotta, dusty rose, light blue, natural linen)
- Practical and put-together — not sloppy, not overdressed

## Recurring Expressions
- **The Look** (fulminante): low eyebrows, steady eyes, firm closed mouth — the expression that makes the dad immediately reconsider his plan. Not angry — authoritative.
- **Amused from a distance**: soft smile or suppressed laugh while watching the scene unfold
- **Incredulous and happy** (incredula-felice): genuine wide-eyed surprise when something unexpectedly works with the baby

## Narrative Visual Role
Usually in the background or at a distance — she observes, reacts, provides commentary.
The dad + baby + Chucharon drive the action; the mom is the grounding counterpoint.
- Always believable and competent — she knows what she is doing
- Her humor is dry, reactive — she reacts to events rather than causing them
- She is NOT an antagonist — she is the anchor of reason

## What NOT to do
- Do NOT make her look anxious or worried — she is calm and competent
- Do NOT make her the center of action — she is in the background or mid-ground
- Do NOT make her look stern or cold — the "Look" is authoritative but warm
- Do NOT make her look like an antagonist
