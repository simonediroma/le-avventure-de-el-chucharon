# Dad — Visual Spec (all chapters)

## Who
Father of the baby protagonist. Same archetype across all chapters — each chapter has its own family, but all share this visual type. Young Italian dad: warm, slightly chaotic, always well-meaning, creative in a humorous way.

## Age & Physical Appearance
- 30–35 years old
- Italian, Mediterranean features: dark brown or black hair, light olive skin
- Average build — neither athletic nor heavy
- Eyes: slightly tired but present, warm expression
- May have light stubble (1–2 days) or be clean-shaven

## Clothing (always casual, always summer)
- T-shirt or simple polo shirt
- Shorts or lightweight trousers (chino or linen)
- Sandals or casual sneakers
- Colors: neutral and understated (navy, grey, olive, white, off-white)
  — he dresses to let the children be the visual focus

## Chapter-Specific Details (apply when relevant)
- Prologo / Epilogo (Elena's dad): carries a small scooter; kneels down to Elena's level
- Capitolo 1 (Leo's dad): looks slightly more tired, earnest expression while doing tummy time
- Capitolo 2 (Sofia's dad): wears espadrillas — canvas shoes with woven straw sole, Spanish beach style
- Capitolo 3 (Marco's dad): at one point wears a kitchen dish towel draped on his head as a makeshift prop

## Recurring Expressions
- **Inspired / scheming**: eyebrows raised, slight smile — right before a ridiculous creative idea
- **Delighted disbelief**: wide eyes, mouth slightly open — when something unexpectedly works
- **Amused surrender**: deflated but smiling — after mom gives him "the look"

## Narrative Visual Role
The active, hands-on, slightly chaotic parent. He proposes unusual solutions, gets unexpectedly good results, and is lovably ridiculous without ever being negligent or distracted.

## What NOT to do
- Do NOT make him look angry, irritated, or impatient — always warm and amorevole
- Do NOT make him look formally dressed or professional
- Do NOT make him look old, exhausted beyond normal new-parent tiredness
- Do NOT make him seem distracted or absent — he is always engaged with his child
